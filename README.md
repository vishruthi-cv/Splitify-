# Splitify
Splitify is a simple expense tracker designed for group trips. It keeps track of who paid for what and calculates exactly how to settle balances without the headache.

Key Features

   -Detailed Expense Logging: Record expenses by name, total amount, and the       specific person who covered the cost.

   -Flexible Splits: Assign exactly which group members were involved in each      transaction.

   -Automatic Calculations: Spending Totals: Tracks the gross amount spent by      every individual.

  -Net Balances: Instantly calculates if a person is "Owed" (positive             balance) or "Owes" (negative balance).

  -Data Persistence: Integrated with localStorage to ensure your trip data        remains safe even after closing the tab or refreshing the page.
